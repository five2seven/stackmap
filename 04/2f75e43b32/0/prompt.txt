Use unit and component tests where appropriate.

2. Create a service with only a name.
3. Create a complete service assigned to a host.
4. Edit and retire a service.
5. Permanently delete a service after confirmation.
6. Prevent deletion of a referenced host.
7. Detect a duplicate host-port conflict.
8. Search and filter services.

## Phase 4: Usability review

Review the app as a first-time homelab user with limited free time.

Identify:

- Labels that are unclear
- Actions that are difficult to find
- Forms that feel unnecessarily long
- Missing feedback after save, import, export, retire, or delete
- Warnings that are too subtle
- Destructive actions that are too easy
- Screens that require explanation
- Areas where incomplete information is treated poorly
- Anything that feels like documentation work rather than a helpful tool

Separate findings into:

- Blocking issue
- Important improvement
- Minor improvement
- Future enhancement outside the MVP

Do not expand the MVP while fixing usability problems.
